## #610 VIRAL VIDEO PROMPT: GIANT SWORD!

*2026-07-24 — [source](https://sifuyik.substack.com/p/610-viral-video-prompt-giant-sword)*

"First Frame Image Prompt
View from the wooden deck of a ship, looking out over the open ocean. In the distance, a massive, ornate, cylindrical sword hilt rises vertically from the water, partially submerged, with intricate metallic details and subtle engravings catching the light. The sky is partly cloudy with soft sunlight breaking through. The ship's deck in the foreground has white railings and a wooden handrail with a teal accent line. The ocean is deep blue with gentle waves stretching to the horizon. Photorealistic CGI, cinematic, epic scale, bright natural lighting, cool color palette dominated by blue ocean and sky, warm wooden deck tones.

Image-to-Video Prompt
Theme: A colossal divine hand draws a giant ornate sword from the ocean, witnessed from the deck of a passing ship

Visuals: Open ocean under a partly cloudy sky, wooden ship deck with white railings and a wooden handrail in the foreground, a massive cylindrical sword hilt rising from the water in the distance, a gigantic metallic hand descending from the clouds, ornate crossguard with intricate detailing, dark grey blade, water cascading heavily off the sword, a massive crashing wave created by the blade's displacement

Camera: Fixed handheld perspective from a moving ship, slight continuous rightward pan, keeping the central action centered as the vessel passes by, no cuts

Style: Photorealistic CGI, cinematic epic fantasy, surreal monumental scale, bright natural daylight, cool blue ocean and sky, metallic silver and grey for the sword and hand, warm wooden deck tones, high detail

Action + Sound Design:

A gigantic metallic hand descends from the sky and firmly grasps the ornate hilt — deep resonant metallic rumble + gentle ocean waves against the ship

The hand begins to pull the sword upward, the ornate crossguard breaking the surface as water cascades heavily off its sides — thunderous water displacement + groaning metal

The massive dark blade continues rising from the ocean, displacing an enormous volume of water and creating a colossal crashing wave — roaring water + powerful sweeping whoosh

The sword is lifted entirely out of frame, leaving the massive wave churning in its wake — booming wave crash + wind rushing across the deck"
