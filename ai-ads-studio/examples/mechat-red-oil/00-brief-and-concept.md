# Flagship Example — زيت المشاط للشعر · Brief & Locked Concept

> The shared creative foundation every format in this example (image / video / 3D /
> landing) is built on. Grounded in the REAL product (products/mechat-red-oil/
> product-lock.yaml) and the researched Gulf norms (knowledge/gulf-beauty-brand-norms.md).
> Nothing here invents claims beyond the landing page.

## 1. Product truth (from the locked registry — never altered)
- Cylindrical tall/slim clear PET bottle, white ribbed screw cap, **brown-amber** oil,
  visible fill-line + headspace.
- Label: white, diagonal **red swoosh + gold pinstripe**, gold «100% natural / طبيعي»
  seal on the **left**, brand lockup **«زيت المشاط للشعر»** in red, benefit lines
  «تركيبة طبيعية / تقوي الشعر / تغذيه وتكثيف الشعر», **«250 ml»**.
- ⚠️ The word **«الأحمر» is NOT on the label** (it's marketing/name only). The bottle is
  never re-labelled. All product pixels come from the real magenta-chroma cutout (route R1).

## 2. Audience & insight
- **Who:** Saudi women 22–45 (primary), GCC secondary; value natural/heritage haircare;
  fight hair loss, want length, density, shine.
- **Awareness:** problem- & solution-aware (they know herbal Mashat oils; the question is
  *which one*, and *does it really work*).
- **Dominant emotion:** trust in a lived, authentic ritual — "the oil my grandmother would
  approve of," modernised. Not glamour spectacle; **quiet confidence + belonging.**

## 3. The lane (from Gulf brand-norms research)
Tradition **(B) Saudi/Gulf-national heritage** — earth-tone, ritual, ingredient-forward,
**model-presence = hands + hair + back-of-head, no face** as the *house style*, not a
workaround. Avoid the imported South-Asian glamour-face look (reads expat/cheap) and any
desert/camel/souk orientalism. Heritage = **home, family, «جدتي»** — lived, current.

## 4. LOCKED CONCEPT — «لمسة جدّتي» / "Grandmother's Touch"
A warm, amber-lit **ritual**: a woman's hands warm the red-amber oil between her palms,
part a thick dark section of hair from **behind/side (no face)**, and work the oil in with
slow, deliberate care — hibiscus & henna resting on the wooden vanity, the real bottle
present and in focus. The feeling: an inherited ritual, done tonight, in a real home.

- **Palette:** warm amber / copper / terracotta + deep wood + brass; NOT clinical white.
- **Setting:** home vanity / majlis corner, warm practical light (lamp, golden hour).
- **Ingredients on camera:** real hibiscus flowers, henna powder, the oil's sheen — tactile.
- **Hero color:** the oil's own brown-amber-red, echoed in light and backdrop.
- **Model presence:** hands + hair (back/side), **no face**. (Optional separate UAE cut may
  add a ¾ face — not in the KSA default.)

## 5. Why THIS concept (scored vs alternatives)
Beat 20 concepts on the 10-axis rubric. Top reasons:
- **Realism/undetectability:** hands + oil + hair on a home surface is the *easiest scene to
  make indistinguishable from real footage* (few faces, real materials, motivated light) —
  serves prime-directive #1.
- **Culture fit (highest):** matches the documented Saudi-heritage category house style;
  zero modesty risk; anti-stereotype.
- **Conversion:** ritual demonstration + ingredient proof is the format that outperforms
  glamour stills on TikTok/Snap (research obs. #8); the bottle stays hero → shoppable.
- **Cost:** static-image-first; the same locked keyframe feeds the video (i2v) and the
  banners (one master → many) → minimum Kie spend.

## 6. Offer & CTA (from the landing page — unchanged)
3-bottle pack **139 SAR** (was 185, −25%), free shipping, cash on delivery (COD).
CTA: «اطلبي الآن · الدفع عند الاستلام» (Order now · Pay on delivery).

## 7. Copy system (Arabic)
- **On-screen overlays: MSA (فصحى)** — elegant, correct, El Messiri display / Tajawal text,
  added in POST (never in-model).
- **Voice-over (video): Khaleeji female** — warm, native, first-person ritual.
- Headlines pool (overlays):
  - «سرّ شعر جدّاتنا… في زجاجة واحدة»  (the secret of our grandmothers' hair — in one bottle)
  - «زيت المشاط الأحمر — تركيبة طبيعية تُطيل، تُكثّف، تُغذّي»
  - «٣ زجاجات ٱليوم بـ ١٣٩ ريال — الدفع عند الاستلام»
  - CTA: «اطلبي الآن»

## 8. Master negative (applies to every asset — see quality/ + skill 24)
No square/round-wrong bottle; no bright ruby oil; no «الأحمر» on the label; no garbled
Arabic; no seal moved/removed; no extra fingers/waxy skin/plastic look; no floating
objects; no desert/camel/souk; no exposed-hair face in the KSA cut; no CGI-clean sterile
look; no text baked by the model.

## 9. Fidelity + quality gates (before ANY spend)
- **Product fidelity (skill 41):** ≥95, no hard-fail, vs products/mechat-red-oil/references.
- **Undetectability (skill 46):** no AI tells.
- **Culture (quality/culture-checklist):** house-style + anti-stereotype pass.
- **Overall (quality/scoring-rubric):** ≥95, no axis <90 → only then compile the Kie call.
