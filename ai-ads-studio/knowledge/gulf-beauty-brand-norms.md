# Gulf Beauty & Hair-Oil Advertising Norms (anti-stereotype, market-grounded)

> **Why this doc exists.** The owner's directive: *don't guess model-presence — match
> what established brands of THIS category actually do in the Gulf, to avoid
> stereotypes.* This doc encodes researched, source-tied norms for how natural
> hair-oil / haircare brands advertise to Saudi (primary) and GCC audiences. Every
> creative decision (model presence, framing, palette, framing of hair, platform cut)
> defers to this doc + the active market culture doc. Consumed by skills 04, 05, 08,
> 09/10, 20, 21, and the creative-matrix planner (30).

## 0. The one-line policy
**Default house style = hands + hair + back-of-head, NO face — owned as *the* aesthetic,
not used as a censorship patch.** Sell the **ritual and the feeling**, not a result-on-a-face.
Lean into **heritage, ingredients, and the oil's real amber-red color**. This is exactly
how the authentic in-category Saudi/Gulf brands already look.

## 1. The category reality — pick the right tradition
There are TWO visual traditions in the Mashat/herbal hair-oil space. Choose deliberately.

- **(A) Expat / pharmacy-channel, South-Asian-heritage style** — Dabur Amla, Bio Amla,
  Parachute, Hemani. Full forward-facing actress, flowing loose uncovered hair,
  Bollywood-adjacent staging. Aimed at price-sensitive/expat shoppers. **To a
  Saudi-national viewer this reads as a *different, cheaper category* — AVOID it.**
- **(B) Saudi/Gulf-national heritage style** — Ghosn Henna ("Al-Mashat Heritage Hair
  Blend"), Diggn'It (Jeddah "Arabian Hair Oil"), Kikiluna ("Red Mashat Khaliji").
  Earth-tone, ritual, ingredient-forward, heritage-word packaging & copy ("traditional
  method", "family-friendly", multi-hour ritual). Often **model-led imagery is absent
  entirely** — the ritual and ingredients ARE the hero. **← This is our lane.**

زيت المشاط الأحمر is a Saudi-heritage red/amber Mashat oil → **tradition (B)**.

## 2. Model-presence policy (the core answer)
| Mode | Use | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Hands + hair, no face** | **DEFAULT** for KSA/primary | Parting sections, oil dripping, scalp massage, braiding/wrapping. Category-authentic. |
| **Back-of-head / ¾ silhouette** | hero / finishing beat | Documented Saudi hair-ad technique: hair movement & shine shown from behind — a *considered cinematic choice*, shot with full beauty-hero lighting, never an awkward crop. |
| **Hijab-as-styling moment** | optional hero | "Fashionable hijab" as the payoff/proxy for the result (Sunsilk's documented solution) — hijab wrapped over freshly-oiled hair. Treat the hijab as a beauty element, confidently lit. |
| **Full face, uncovered hair** | **exception only** | Reserve for a **separate UAE-facing cut** (UAE tolerates more). Never the KSA default. |

**How to show hair authentically without breaking modesty:** show the *ritual*, not the
*result-on-a-face* — texture and motion (oil sheen, hair swinging back-of-head, steam
from a warm towel) as the "proof" beat; length/shine as a **detail shot** (ends held in a
hand, hair spilling over a shoulder from behind), not a turn-to-camera reveal.

## 3. Aesthetic (what "premium & native" looks like here)
- **Palette:** warm **amber / copper / terracotta** (matches the oil's real color — use it,
  don't fight it), deep wood, brass/gold vessel details. **Not** cold clinical studio-white.
- **Ingredients on camera:** whole herbs, henna, hibiscus, powders, sidr leaves — tactile,
  real texture, **not CGI-clean**.
- **Setting:** **home > studio** — majlis / bedroom vanity with warm practical light reads
  as authentic ritual, not staged.
- **Typography:** naskh or modern-heritage Arabic lettering (El Messiri display / Tajawal
  text), never generic Latin sans dropped into Arabic.
- **Pacing:** slower ritual pacing for Instagram/hero; punchier tutorial cuts for TikTok/Snap.

## 4. Heritage framing (do it right, not orientalist)
- **Right:** a rediscovered/modernized **grandmother's ritual** («جدتي» / heritage lineage) —
  lived, current, personal, family, home.
- **Wrong:** "ancient mystical Arabia," desert/camel/tent/souk backdrops as shorthand for
  heritage. The region's own creative press actively rejects this. Heritage = home &
  family & ritual, **not** landscape tourism.

## 5. Platform mapping (KSA)
- **Snapchat** — highest engagement for young beauty (≈90% of 13–34; top per-capita open
  rate globally). Format: **tutorial / ritual-demonstration** vertical video.
- **TikTok** — widest reach (~138% of adult population). Format: punchy tutorial/demo cuts.
- **Instagram** — the "aspiration layer"; Reels tutorials outperform static posts 3–5×.
  Format: polished aspirational ritual visuals + Reels.

## 6. Emotional register
Personal narrative + **product-as-feeling** beats beauty-shot spectacle. Ground copy/VO in
a **specific, believable woman's specific routine** ("what you feel from the inside"),
not generic "beauty"/"empowerment" claims.

## 7. DO / DON'T (hard guardrails)
**DO**
1. Default to hands / hair / back-of-head framing — own it as the house style.
2. Let the oil's amber-red be the hero color throughout (bottle, grade, backdrop).
3. Ground copy/VO in a specific woman's specific ritual, not generic beauty claims.
4. Match format to platform (tutorial for TikTok/Snap; aspirational for Instagram).
5. If a hijab appears, style it as a beauty element (confident, well-lit), not avoided.

**DON'T**
1. Don't use imported South-Asian glamour-face / loose-hair staging (reads expat/cheap).
2. Don't use desert / camel / tent / souk "exotic" backdrops — orientalist cliché.
3. Don't force a face-forward hero reveal "for impact" — unnecessary for performance,
   and reads as compliance-unaware to a KSA audience.
4. Don't write generic "empowerment" VO detached from a real, specific woman's life —
   the most-mocked current MENA cliché.
5. Don't treat modesty as apologetic (blurred faces, awkward crops) — treat framing
   choices with full beauty-hero production quality.
