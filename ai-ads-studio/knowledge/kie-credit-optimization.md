# KIE Credit-Optimization Playbook

> **Role in the framework:** the operational doctrine behind the credit engine.
> `config/kie-models.yaml` is the *catalog* (which model, what it costs, what it
> can do); **this file is the *discipline*** (how to spend as little as possible
> to ship the same quality). Every producer skill (30 creative-matrix-planner,
> 31 format-router, 29 cost-optimizer, 38 upscale-finisher, and all directors
> 32–36) enforces the ten policies below. When a director wants to spend a credit
> and this playbook says "not yet," this playbook wins.
>
> **Prime-directive inheritance (STUDIO-BIBLE §0, V2 §0):** Realism first,
> Conversion second, **Cost third — but cost is a *first-class routed decision*
> per asset.** Claude reasons, scores, and QA's *fully* before any credit is
> spent. The target is not "cheap." The target is **one paid final render per
> approved concept, and zero wasted regenerations.**
>
> ⚠️ **Pricing drifts.** Kie (kie.ai) is an aggregator; its line-up and per-call
> credit prices change often. This doc reasons in **relative cost tiers**
> (`draft ≈ 1`, `iterate ≈ 3`, `final ≈ 10` — see `kie-models.yaml:cost_tiers`),
> never authoritative prices. Every worked example below is **order-of-magnitude
> planning**. Read live pricing from kie.ai before any paid run (`verify_live`).

## 0. The core mental model — where credits actually leak

Credits are almost never lost on the *final* render. They leak in the **iteration
loop before it** and in **regenerating things that never needed regenerating**.
Four leak sources account for nearly all waste in an AI ad studio:

1. **Regenerating for Arabic text.** The model spells زيت المشاط wrong, you regen,
   it invents «الأحمر» on the label (a Bible-forbidden drift), you regen again.
   This is the **#1 credit sink** and it is 100% avoidable — text goes in POST.
2. **Iterating at final tier.** Running Veo3 / flux-pro / gpt-image (tier ≈ 10)
   while you are still deciding composition. Every decision made at final tier
   costs ~10× the same decision made at draft tier.
3. **Re-generating for a new size/ratio.** Making a fresh generation for the 1:1,
   the 4:5, the 9:16, the 300×250 banner — instead of cropping ONE master.
4. **Product drift → discard.** A text-to-image of the product comes back with the
   wrong cap or a redrawn label; it is unusable, so the whole spend is wasted.
   Reference-conditioning (R1/R2/R3) removes this entire class of loss.

Every policy below attacks one or more of these four leaks. Internalize the leaks
and the policies become obvious.

## 1. The Ten Policies

These are canonical (V2 §5). Each has a one-line rule, the leak it plugs, and a
concrete Gulf-hair-oil application.

### Policy 1 — REASON BEFORE SPEND
**Rule:** No model is called until every upstream quality gate is green — concept
scored, brand/product lock applied, cultural QA passed (`gulf-beauty-brand-norms`),
negatives compiled, fidelity checklist ready. Claude behaves like a $500k agency
that has already storyboarded, then spends like a startup on the single render.
**Leak plugged:** all four — thinking is free, generations are not.
**Application:** before generating the hero keyframe of the Red Mechat oil, the
concept is fully written (shot, light recipe, oil viscosity note, model styling per
Gulf norms), so the paid render executes a *decided* image, not an exploration.

### Policy 2 — IMAGE-FIRST
**Rule:** Never open with video. Lock the hero **keyframe** on a cheap/fast image
model (nano-banana / seedream, tier ≈ 1). Only the **approved** keyframe is
animated (image→video, route R3). One video generation per approved hero.
**Leak plugged:** #2 (video is the most expensive tier; deciding in video is ruinous).
**Application:** the 8s garnet-oil-pour hero is composed, QC'd and fidelity-checked
as a still first; Veo3 is called once, on the frame everyone already approved.
**Math:** deciding a hero over 6 iterations in video ≈ 6 × 10 = 60 credits. Deciding
it in image (6 × 1) then one video (10) = 16 credits. **~73% saved, same result.**

### Policy 3 — TEXT IN POST
**Rule:** Arabic headline / CTA / price / label copy is **never** generated by the
model. It is typeset in post by skill 37 (typography-compositor) in El Messiri /
Tajawal, RTL-correct, with LTR-safe numerals. The model renders a clean plate with
reserved negative-space overlay zones.
**Leak plugged:** #1 (the biggest sink) — and it *guarantees* correct Arabic, which
no image model reliably does (`kie-models.yaml`: every model `arabic_text: poor`).
**Application:** «سرّ نعومة شعرك» and the CTA button are composited after render.
The bottle's physical label «زيت المشاط للشعر» is **preserved by reference**, never
re-lettered — protecting against the forbidden «الأحمر» drift (product-lock).

### Policy 4 — ONE MASTER → MANY EXPORTS
**Rule:** One high-res master per concept. Every ratio (1:1, 4:5, 9:16) and every
banner size (feed 1080², story 1080×1920, IAB 300×250 / 728×90 / 160×600 / 320×50)
is a **crop / recompose** of that master (skills 33 + 38), never a new generation.
**Leak plugged:** #3.
**Application:** the static image-ad master is shot with headroom and side margin so
the same file crops cleanly to Meta feed, Story, and the retargeting banner set.
**Math:** naive = 1 gen per size. 6 placements × 3 iterations × tier-1 = 18 credits,
plus you now have 6 *inconsistent* bottles. One-master = 6 iterations to lock the
master (6) + 1 final (10) + 0 for crops = 16 credits, one consistent bottle, and
crops are free forever. Add a 7th size later: **0 credits** vs another full gen.

### Policy 5 — DRAFT → FINAL GATING
**Rule:** Iterate exclusively on **draft tier** (low res / fewer steps / fast model,
tier ≈ 1). When QC ≥ 95 (quality rubric) **and** fidelity gate passes (skill 41),
spend **final tier ONCE** — one final render or one upscale. **Never iterate at
final cost.**
**Leak plugged:** #2.
**Application:** all garnet-oil lighting and hair-flow exploration happens on
nano-banana drafts; flux-kontext (final fidelity) is called on the single winner.
**Math:** 8 iterations at final (8 × 10 = 80) vs 8 drafts + 1 final (8 + 10 = 18).
**~77% saved.** The rule of thumb: **if you are still changing something, you are
still in draft.**

### Policy 6 — REFERENCE + SEED LOCK
**Rule:** Reuse the **product reference image** (the real packshot,
`products/mechat-red-oil/references/01-front-white.png`) **plus a fixed seed** across
the entire asset set. One consistent bottle identity → far fewer discards.
**Leak plugged:** #4 (product drift). This is also the fidelity backbone: routes
R1/R2/R3 all condition on the real photo, so the product is *never reinvented*.
**Application:** every image and every keyframe for the campaign feeds the same
front packshot as reference and holds one seed, so the cap, seal, fill-line and
label are identical bottle-to-bottle. The router is **forbidden** from picking a
`t2i` model for any product-bearing asset (`kie-models.yaml:routing_matrix`).
**Math:** without reference-lock, ~1 in 3 product gens drifts (wrong cap / redrawn
label) and is discarded → you pay ~1.5× for every usable frame. With reference-lock,
drift ≈ 0 → you pay 1×. On a 12-asset campaign that is ~6 fully wasted generations
you never make.

### Policy 7 — BATCH SHARED-SCENE ASSETS
**Rule:** Landing-page section images that share a look (ingredient cards, benefit
tiles, trust sections) share **style tokens + seed** and are produced as one batch,
not one-off.
**Leak plugged:** #2 + #4 (consistency without per-image re-exploration).
**Application:** the 5 LP ingredient/benefit cards (argan macro, shine sweep, scalp
close-up, before/after slot, texture pour) are batched on seedream with one shared
style string → one coherent set, minimal iteration, R1-batch route.
**Math:** 5 sections × 4 solo iterations each (20 draft credits) + inconsistent look
vs 1 shared style locked in 4 iterations then 5 batched draws (4 + 5 = 9) → **~55%
saved** and a matching set.

### Policy 8 — SHORT LOOPABLE MICROVIDEO
**Rule:** LP motion = **3–5s seamless loops** on the fast/cheap video tier
(hailuo / seedance / veo3-fast / kling), first frame = last frame. **Not** 8s hero
renders. Route R3 from an existing/locked keyframe.
**Leak plugged:** #2 (video length and tier are the cost drivers).
**Application:** the ambient oil-pour and bottle slow-rotate loops behind LP sections
are 4s, looped, muted-autoplay — a fraction of a hero's cost, and they run forever.
**Math:** an 8s Veo3 loop (tier ≈ 10) vs a 4s seedance/hailuo loop (tier ≈ 1 draft):
**~90% cheaper** for background motion the viewer never studies frame-by-frame.

### Policy 9 — UPSCALE, DON'T REGENERATE
**Rule:** Need more resolution? **Upscale** the approved render (kie-upscaler, tier
≈ 1), never re-generate at higher settings (which re-rolls composition *and* risks
product drift).
**Leak plugged:** #3 + #4.
**Application:** the approved 1024px hero draft is upscaled to delivery resolution;
we do not re-run flux-pro at 2K and gamble the label.
**Math:** re-gen at final for resolution (10, and a drift risk) vs upscale the keeper
(≈ 1, zero drift). **~90% saved** and the composition you approved is preserved
pixel-for-pixel.

### Policy 10 — SURFACE THE BILL
**Rule:** Before **any** spend, emit a per-asset credit estimate **and** a campaign
total, each line carrying a **cheaper fallback route**. QC gate (≥ 95) + fidelity
gate before every final-tier spend. No production starts without an approved plan +
budget (skill 30 gate).
**Leak plugged:** all four — visibility is the meta-control that keeps 1–9 honest.
**Application:** skill 30 hands the owner a table: each asset, its route (R1/R2/R3),
draft+final credit estimate, fallback (e.g. "final on flux-kontext; fallback
nano-banana + upscale"), and the total with a **naive-vs-optimized savings figure**
(see the example in `examples/mechat-red-oil/25-kie-credit-plan.md`).

## 2. The routing shorthand (pairs with `kie-models.yaml`)

The playbook decides *when* to spend; the catalog decides *which model*. The bridge:

| Stage | Tier | Typical model (image) | Typical model (video) | Spend rule |
|-------|------|-----------------------|-----------------------|------------|
| Explore / compose | draft ≈ 1 | nano-banana, seedream | hailuo, seedance | iterate freely |
| Near-final check | iterate ≈ 3 | flux-kontext, imagen | veo3-fast, kling | 1–2 confirmations |
| The ONE keeper | final ≈ 10 | flux-kontext, gpt-image | veo3, runway-gen | spend ONCE, gated |
| Resolution | draft ≈ 1 | kie-upscaler | — | upscale, never re-gen |

**Hard rule carried from `kie-models.yaml`:** any asset whose subject is the product
uses a reference-capable route (`i2i / edit / inpaint / bg-replace / i2v`). `t2i` /
`t2v` of the product is **forbidden** — that is Policy 6 as a routing constraint.

## 4. Worked campaign math — naive vs optimized

A representative Red Mechat launch: **1 hero video, 3 static image ads, 1 banner
system (6 sizes), 5 LP section images, 2 LP microvideo loops.** Tiers per
`kie-models.yaml` (draft ≈ 1, iterate ≈ 3, final ≈ 10). Numbers are
order-of-magnitude planning, not prices.

### 4a. Naive studio (no discipline)

| Asset | What the naive studio does | Credits |
|-------|----------------------------|--------:|
| Hero video | Explore 6× directly in Veo3 (6 × 10) | 60 |
| 3 image ads | 3 concepts × 5 iterations, all at final tier (15 × 10) | 150 |
| Banner ×6 sizes | 1 fresh generation per size × 3 iterations (18 × 10) | 180 |
| 5 LP sections | 5 × 4 solo iterations at final (20 × 10) | 200 |
| 2 microvideos | 2 × 8s Veo3 loops × 3 tries (6 × 10) | 60 |
| Arabic regens | text baked in-model → ~8 extra regens (8 × 10) | 80 |
| Product drift discards | ~1/3 of product gens redrawn wrong (~10 × 10) | 100 |
| **TOTAL (naive)** | | **≈ 830** |

### 4b. Optimized studio (this playbook)

| Asset | What the disciplined studio does | Credits |
|-------|----------------------------------|--------:|
| Hero video | Keyframe: 6 drafts (6). One Veo3 final (10). Image-first, P2 | 16 |
| 3 image ads | 3 masters, ~4 drafts each (12). 3 finals (3 × 10) | 42 |
| Banner ×6 sizes | Crop/recompose ONE approved image master. P4 | 0 |
| 5 LP sections | Batched shared style: 4 drafts to lock + 5 draws (9). P7 | 9 |
| 2 microvideos | 2 × 4s cheap-tier loops from locked keyframes (2 × 1). P8 | 2 |
| Arabic regens | 0 — all text in post (skill 37). P3 | 0 |
| Product drift discards | ≈ 0 — reference + seed lock. P6 | 0 |
| Upscales to delivery res | 4 keepers upscaled (4 × 1). P9 | 4 |
| **TOTAL (optimized)** | | **≈ 73** |

**Result: ≈ 830 → ≈ 73 credits, roughly a 91% reduction — for the same or better,
more *consistent* deliverables** (one bottle identity everywhere, guaranteed-correct
Arabic, no drift discards). The banner system alone (180 → 0) and the two avoidance
policies (Arabic regens 80 → 0, drift 100 → 0) do most of the heavy lifting; they
cost *nothing but discipline*.

### 4c. The savings, ranked by leverage

1. **One-master-many-exports (P4):** biggest single line — turns N sizes into 1 gen.
2. **Text-in-post (P3):** eliminates the #1 regen sink outright.
3. **Reference+seed lock (P6):** eliminates drift discards *and* guarantees fidelity.
4. **Draft→final gating (P5) + image-first (P2):** shrinks every remaining spend ~5×.
5. **Short loops (P8) + upscale (P9):** trim the tail.

## 5. Anti-patterns (never do these)

- **Deciding in video.** Any composition/lighting choice made in a video model is a
  ~10× overpayment. Decide in image, animate the winner.
- **"Just regenerate, it'll spell it right this time."** It won't (`arabic_text:
  poor`, universally). Fix it in post — it is *free and correct*.
- **A fresh generation per aspect ratio.** Crop the master. A 7th ratio next month
  must also cost 0.
- **Iterating on flux-pro / gpt-image / Veo3 / runway.** Those are final-tier. If
  you are still changing something, you are in draft — move back to nano-banana /
  seedream / hailuo.
- **Text-to-image of the bottle "to save a step."** It drifts (wrong cap, redrawn
  label, invented «الأحمر») and the whole spend is discarded. Always R1/R2/R3.
- **Re-generating at higher settings for resolution.** Upscale the keeper; re-gen
  re-rolls composition and risks the label.
- **Spending before the bill is surfaced.** No plan + budget approval (skill 30
  gate) = no production. Silent spend is a process failure, not just a cost one.
- **Turntable / full-rotation 3D from a single front photo.** It will drift
  (V2 §7bis.1 3D caveat). Restrict to a label-face-on micro-move and FLAG that more
  reference angles unlock rotation.

## 6. Fallback ladder (when the plan must get cheaper)

Skill 30 surfaces a fallback per asset; here is the standard ladder, cheapest sacrifice
of quality first, so a budget cut is a *routed* decision, not a panic:

1. **Final image model → cheaper final.** flux-kontext → nano-banana + upscale.
   Small fidelity cost, large credit save.
2. **Hero video tier down.** veo3 → veo3-fast or kling for the final animate; keep
   image-first keyframe discipline. Re-QC the temporal-consistency axis (skill 45).
3. **Cut microvideo count, not length below loopable.** Fewer loops beats broken
   loops; a non-seamless loop reads as AI (fails skill 46).
4. **Reduce variant count, never the fidelity/QC gates.** Ship 2 strong image-ad
   variants instead of 4 mediocre ones. Gates (QC ≥ 95, fidelity, undetectability)
   are **never** the thing that gets cut — a cheap ad that reads as AI has negative
   ROAS.
5. **Defer, don't degrade.** If quality can't be met in budget, the plan says
   "defer this asset," it does not ship a fail. Realism is priority #1, above cost.

**Never trade below the floor:** fidelity gate (skill 41), undetectability gate
(skill 46), and cultural QA (`gulf-beauty-brand-norms`) are hard floors. Cost
optimization operates *above* them, never through them.

## 7. Enforcement hooks (who runs this, where)

- **30 creative-matrix-planner** — owns Policy 10; will not release a plan without
  per-asset + total budget, fallback per line, and the naive-vs-optimized delta.
- **31 format-router** — owns Policies 2, 4, 6, 8; maps each asset to route + model +
  tier via `kie-models.yaml`; forbidden from `t2i` on product assets.
- **29 cost-optimizer** — cross-checks the whole plan against Policies 1–10 before
  spend; the last gate before the bill is surfaced.
- **37 typography-compositor** — is Policy 3 in skill form (Arabic in post).
- **38 upscale-finisher** — owns Policies 5 and 9 (draft→final, upscale-not-regen)
  and the one-master export matrix (Policy 4).
- **41 product-fidelity-checker** — the automatic gate that makes Policy 6 pay off:
  rejects drift before any final spend, on every asset, every product.
- **27 quality-scorer / 46 ai-tell-detector** — the QC ≥ 95 and undetectability
  floors that gate every final-tier credit.

**Bottom line:** the studio's job is to make every generation *count* — reason first,
build cheap, prove it green, then spend once. One approved concept → one paid final
render → infinite free exports. That is the whole discipline.
